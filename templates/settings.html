How to get your free Gemini API key:

  1. 1

    Open Google AI Studio: Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with your Google account.

  2. 2

    Create Key: Click the blue "Create API Key" button.

  3. 3

    Select Project: Choose "Create key in new project" (or select any existing Google Cloud project).

  4. 4

    Copy & Paste: Copy your key (starts with AIzaSy...), paste it in the field below, and click "Save Gemini key".
